# SockNinja Privacy Policy

_Effective: November 2, 2026_

SockNinja finds socks in your photos and helps you sort them into pairs. It is built so that your
photos stay on your iPhone.

## What SockNinja does on your device

- **Camera.** SockNinja uses the camera to show live sock detection and to take photos of your socks.
  The camera feed and photos are processed **on your iPhone**. They are never uploaded.
- **Photos you choose.** If you import a photo, iOS's photo picker gives SockNinja only the photo you
  pick. SockNinja does not read the rest of your library.
- **Saving an image.** If you tap Save Image in the share sheet, SockNinja asks permission to add
  that one image to your photo library.
- **History.** Scan results, your pair corrections and game results are stored **locally on your
  device**, so you can look at them again. You can delete them at any time in
  Settings → Clear All History. Deleting the app also deletes them.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** SockNinja:

- has no accounts and no sign-in;
- does not send your photos, scans or any other data to us or to anyone else;
- contains no advertising, analytics or tracking services, and no third-party SDKs;
- does not track you across apps or websites.

If you choose to share an image with the iOS share sheet, it goes only where you send it, under that
service's own privacy terms.

## Children

SockNinja collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If a
future version of the app ever collects data, this policy and the App Store privacy label will be
updated before that version is released.
