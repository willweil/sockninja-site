# SockNinja Support

Need help? Email **sockninja.support@gmail.com**. Please include your iPhone model, your iOS version and, if you can, a screenshot.

## Tips for good results

- Lay socks flat on a plain, evenly lit surface, with a little space between them.
- Hold the phone steady. In Live mode a box appears after about half a second of steady detection.
- Busy, patterned backgrounds and sock-like objects make mistakes more likely.

## Known limitations

- **Things that aren't socks.** SockNinja sometimes puts a box on something that isn't a sock, such
  as a paper towel, a tripod or building toys. Tap the box, then tap **Not a sock** to remove it:
  - in Live mode, it stays hidden while it's in view, and **Show hidden** brings it back;
  - in a photo result, it is removed from that result, and you can **Undo**.
- **Pair suggestions.** Pairs are suggestions. Similar patterns, such as dots and stripes, can be
  paired wrongly. Tap a sock and then its real mate to re-pair them, or unpair them. Then confirm
  the result.
- **Outlines.** Sometimes a sock's outline doesn't cover the whole sock.

## Your data

Everything stays on your iPhone. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy). To delete your history, go to
Settings → Clear All History.
